I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group led by Professor Junxian He. Professor Junxian He is my PhD supervisor, and he also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), from which I graduated in June 2024.

## Skills & Research Interests

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
